# SKILL ISSUE: creative direction

**Logline.** A student tries to learn AI faster than AI can change. He loses that race,
and then finds out the race is a product.

**Interpretation of speed.** Technology accelerates, and the people in it are told to
accelerate too. The film never shows AI as a villain. The student keeps building his own
treadmill: a notebook that says *LEARN AI*, then *LEARN AI FASTER*, then a machine that
automates the learning and prints an 847-skill roadmap that's already obsolete. The last
shot shows that his bedroom is one station in a looping conveyor system. Thousands of
identical students chase a finish line labelled **CAUGHT UP** that always moves away faster
than they do.

**Tone.** Deadpan cinematic seriousness applied to a tiny personal crisis. The jokes are
visual and work without dialogue. Text only appears where the joke is the text.

## Visual identity

| | Bedroom (Acts I–III) | Hall (Act IV) |
|---|---|---|
| Key light | Warm amber desk lamp (2700 K) | Distant white-gold finish-line glow (backlight) |
| Fill | Cool blue monitor/laptop spill | Dim sodium work lights, cold ambient |
| Accent | Cyan on the agent machine only | Restrained red warning beacons |
| Materials | Worn wood, paper, ceramic, fabric | Desaturated steel, rubber belts, concrete |
| Camera | Locked-off and slow early, increasingly kinetic | Orbit reveal, then a ballistic pull-back |

**Character.** A stylised student in his early twenties: oversized head, mustard hoodie with
drawstrings, dark jeans, white sneakers, round glasses, messy dark hair. The hair gets messier,
the eye bags darker and the eyelids heavier as the `stress` channel rises. Expression comes
from big eyes, brows, lids, a shape-keyed mouth, head/neck posture and readable hand poses.

**The window is fake.** It's a backlit city panel, and it brightens to a cold dawn as the clock
races. In Act IV it turns out to be a light box on the back of a stage flat.

## Sound arc

Room tone → keyboard → notification dings that get more frequent → a rhythmic layer whose
tempo comes from the speed curve → a dead stop on *AI CAN NOW DO THAT* → a stab on the scream →
the machine's mechanical activation and hum → paper-feed chatter → **near silence** on
*ROADMAP OBSOLETE* → footsteps and a whisper → the walls slam down into industrial ambience →
the PA voice → a 10x acceleration → an absurd epic hit on the cut to black.
